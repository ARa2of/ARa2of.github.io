Raouf — 4 Week Program

4 days / week · ~60 min per session · cable-heavy, free-weight anchored
Program notes & how to send edits

The four days

  • Day 1 — The Anvil · Chest / front & side delts / biceps / abs. Pressing is the hammer, curls are the finish. Matches your Raouf base pairing of chest + biceps.
  • Day 2 — The Anchor · Back / rear delts / traps / triceps / abs. Everything that pulls and locks out; biceps stay off this day, triceps finish it.
  • Day 3 — Bedrock · Legs / lower back (deadlift main) / abs. Ground zero — the one day you never skip. Rotation: Deadlift → Back Squat → Trap-Bar → Sumo.
  • Day 4 — Wildcard · Optional mix — arms, pump & conditioning. Drop it entirely on a busy week; it exists to add arm volume and let you leave feeling good.

Design notes

  • Progression: add weight or a rep each week on the first two movements of every day. When you hit the top of the rep range on all sets, increase the load. FST-7 and drop sets are the intensity tools — don't chase failure on heavy compounds.
  • Volume: ~12–18 hard sets per muscle per week, which sits in the current meta-analytic sweet spot (Pelland 2024). FST-7 finishers count toward weekly volume and add the stretch-mediated hypertrophy effect (Maeo 2022).
  • Chest day free-weight rule: exactly one barbell or dumbbell chest movement per week — flat barbell bench → incline barbell bench → flat dumbbell press → incline dumbbell press. Everything else in that day's chest work is cable or machine, which also makes the drop sets and FST-7s easier to set up.
  • Arms: biceps get 7 sets on The Anvil plus a superset on Wildcard; triceps get 7 sets on The Anchor plus the same Wildcard superset — enough direct work without eating into the big lifts.
  • Cables vs free weights: roughly equivalent for hypertrophy in recent meta-analyses; cables give constant tension and easy drop sets, so each day keeps one or two free-weight anchors (bench/incline, barbell or DB row, deadlift/squat, shrugs).
  • Time: warm-up 5–8 min + 6–8 exercises at 45–60s rest lands at roughly 55–65 min.
  • If rushed: abs on Day 4 is the first thing to drop; Day 4 as a whole is optional.
